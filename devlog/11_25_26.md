Finally made it work, as usual, the problem was not the code (except for a constant on the basecharacter), but on the 2735401250 options of the node on Godot. I found out that I hate transformations and offset configurations of the HBoxContainer. Thanks to MarkerTech and Vortex Venturer for helping me seeing that the problem was those options. Setting the boxes was easy, but making them not visible from right to left, without changing the spacing between them was the hard part. So learning of the day and quick note for my future self: don't use offsets nor transformations for these items, use general espacing between objects (Theme Override > Constants > Separation) + filling method (Grow Direction: Horizontal set to Right).
